# ZIWU — The Meridian of Intelligence

**Zero to Infinity. World to Universe.**

**子午：为智能立尺度，向未知拓边界。**

We bring researchers and developers together to measure the frontiers of AI through reproducible evaluation, and explore the real world through scientific, engineering, and robotics tasks.

子午是一个技术驱动的开放型社区。我们连接全球研究者与开发者，把真实问题、可复用的方法与可验证的贡献连接起来。

## What we build

- **AI for Science** — Turn scientific and engineering problems into reproducible, automatically verifiable benchmark tasks, and contribute them for community review.
- **Robotics & Physical AI** — Study how AI agents act in the physical world through simulation and real-robot trials, with public evidence of both successes and failures.
- **Open research** — Connect people through concrete problems, reusable tools, peer review, and verifiable contributions.

Explore our work and its current scope on [ziwutech.com](https://ziwutech.com). Read our [methodology](https://ziwutech.com/methodology/) and [community charter](https://ziwutech.com/charter).

## ZIWU-Bench · Official API evaluation

[ZIWU-Bench](https://ziwutech.com) is our evaluation project for scientific, engineering and robotics tasks. The upcoming Qwen, Kimi and DeepSeek comparison uses their respective **official provider APIs**: Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, Moonshot AI and DeepSeek. Every published result must identify the exact model, official endpoint, harness, task revision and verifiable run evidence.

**Evaluation status:** a new unified cohort is being prepared alongside a GPU resource pool. New comparative scores have not yet been produced; task development and earlier trials are distinct from this cohort.

**中文：** Qwen、Kimi、DeepSeek 的新一轮对比将分别使用各厂商官方 API。每条成绩记录具体型号、官方接口、harness、任务版本和运行证据。GPU 资源池与统一协议正在准备，新批次尚未启动，未实测的项目不会填入分数。
